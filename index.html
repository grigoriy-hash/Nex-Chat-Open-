About voice calls
Voice calling is a new feature on NexChat. We cannot promise it will work on every network or every device. If audio does not connect, or if the call freezes, hangs up, or drops, please send a short report to Grigoriy within the next few days. Mention what device you used, what network you were on, and what went wrong. Your report helps us fix it for everyone.
